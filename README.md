# Mizrachi Insurance Agency reports

Static client reporting site for GOAL account #441. Edition 11-11-26. Reporting period: last 3 months (Aug 10 to Nov 10, 2026), with a June to Nov 10 monthly view.

No build step. Open `index.html`, or deploy the folder as is (see `vercel.json`: clean URLs, security headers, noindex).

## Campaign Reports
- Campaign Overview
- By Producer
- Home Campaign Performance
- Auto Campaign Performance
- Monthly Report by Source
- Source & ZIP Report (with ZIP CSV)
- Inbound Call Review

## Website & Research
- California Demographic Research
- California Wildfire Risk
- California ZIP Data (CSV)

All conversion metrics are calculated on leads, not clicks. No sales are recorded in GOAL for this account yet, so the headline metric is cost per quote.
