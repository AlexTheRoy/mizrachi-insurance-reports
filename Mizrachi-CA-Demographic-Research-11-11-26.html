Why it matters for Home and the bundle
  • FamiliesFamily households are the natural multi-policy household: a home, two or more cars and often teen drivers. Inland Empire and San Joaquin Valley have the highest family shares.
  • Single householdsA one-person household is usually a one-car, price-led quote; clear options at two or three price points keep the call moving.
  • Households 65+Households with someone 65 or older often keep the same carrier for years and value a local agent who reviews discounts and coverage limits each year.
Why it matters for Auto
  • Vehicles per householdStatewide, 62.2% of households keep two or more vehicles and only 7.1% keep none, so nearly every household is also an Auto prospect. A Home quote is the moment to ask about every vehicle at the address.
  • Los Angeles and the Inland EmpireHouseholds in Los Angeles County keep 1.79 vehicles on average and in the Inland Empire 2.13: more cars per policy in the Inland Empire.
  • Spreadsheet columnsFamily, living alone, 65+ and vehicle columns for every ZIP are in the spreadsheet (B11001, B11007, B08201).

Data & files

  • SourceU.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (ACS) 2020-2024 5-year data (released December 2025), table-based summary file, tables B01002, B01003, B08201, B11001, B11007, B19013, B25001, B25002, B25003, B25004, B25077. Geography: 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas.
  • Coverage1,763 populated ZIPs: every California ZIP with resident population whose land is mostly in California (5 border ZIPs mostly in Nevada, Oregon or Arizona are left out). Median income is published for 1,591 ZIPs and median home value for 1,600.
  • Home valueMedian value of owner-occupied housing units (B25077). The regional figure is the middle ZIP value weighted by each ZIP's owner-occupied homes.
  • VehiclesVehicles available per household = (1 × one-vehicle households + 2 × two + 3 × three + 4 × four or more) ÷ households, from B08201. Counting “4 or more” as 4 makes it a slight floor.
  • GeographyEach ZIP is assigned to the county holding the largest share of its land (2020 ZCTA-to-county relationship file). Los Angeles and Orange are their own regions; Riverside and San Bernardino form the Inland Empire; the rest of the state is grouped into eight regions.
  • Leads380 households that requested a quote, households that requested a quote from August 10 to November 10, 2026 (354 Home and 26 Auto), one count per household. 379 matched a populated California ZIP. 368 are marked as homeowners on the lead record. The June 1 to November 10 figures use 1,876 households as context. Only counts by ZIP are used.
  • PrecisionAll values carry ACS margins of error and are most reliable for larger ZIPs. Blank means not published, never zero.
  • FilesMizrachi-CA-ZIP-Data-11-11-26.csv · every ZIP, every column, with the hazard tier and Home and Auto treatments from the companion report and your lead counts by ZIP.
